Scotiabank Arena

Motion system design for downtown Toronto landmark installation reaching millions of annual viewers across pedestrian and highway sightlines. Deployed continuously for 5+ years as permanent brand infrastructure, optimized for LED technical constraints including refresh rates, pixel density, and ambient light.

{% responsiveImage "/assets/motion/ScotiabankArena_Exterior.jpg", "Scotiabank Arena exterior featuring animated LED displays", "fillImage" %}

The animation framework addresses large-scale LED infrastructure constraints while maintaining brand legibility across drastically different viewing contexts—from street-level pedestrians to Gardiner Expressway drivers. Each animation functions as ambient brand presence designed for environmental scale and long-term deployment.
