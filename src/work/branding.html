Vesca Beauty

Created a golden hour-inspired brand identity and visual system for a bronzer line, designing end-to-end packaging architecture spanning the complete customer journey from shipping to retail presentation. Directed destination-themed product photography across three shade collections to reinforce the brand's sun-chasing aesthetic.

Developed brand identity and visual system around "golden hour warmth and destination aesthetics" for a bronzer brand. The concept—"catch Vesca at golden hour, chasing the sun till dusk"—informed all design decisions from packaging to photography, creating sensorial warmth across retail, PR, shipping, and social touchpoints.
