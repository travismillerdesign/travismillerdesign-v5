Vesca Beauty

Created complete brand identity for a new beauty brand, including packaging design and website.

Designed brand identity inspired by golden hour warmth and destination travel. Packaging spanned shipping boxes to retail presentation, with photography and visual language that created a cohesive sun-chasing aesthetic across all customer touchpoints.
