My role

Google Beam uses AI and 3D light field display technologies to enable face-to-face conversations at natural scale. New interaction patterns needed to be invented from scratch for this emerging technology. Working in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment, I established the foundational design system, defining components, spatial behaviors, and motion principles for an entirely new medium while adapting to evolving technical constraints and user research.

Systems Foundation

Built component libraries and design patterns from scratch for emerging technology, establishing scalable foundations that could adapt as product scope evolved. Defined interaction models and visual hierarchy for experiences that required new approaches to spatial design and user presence.

Motion & Interaction Design

Created motion guidelines and interaction specifications using After Effects and ProtoPie to communicate complex behaviors to stakeholders and engineering. Prototyped new interaction patterns to validate design decisions and enable user research studies in a rapidly evolving technical landscape.

Engineering Collaboration

Developed structured specification and handoff processes aligned with engineering workflows, reducing friction between design and development. Created detailed component specs with precise measurements and responsive behavior documentation, ensuring design intent translated accurately to implementation within aggressive timelines.

Cross-Functional Enablement

Built internal tooling and templates that enabled product managers, researchers, and designers to work more efficiently. Created reusable assets and presentation frameworks that reduced repetitive work and improved stakeholder communication across the organization.
