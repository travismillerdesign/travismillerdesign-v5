Apple

Scalable component infrastructure for apple.com

Case Study —

My role

As an embedded designer supporting 20+ product teams across apple.com, I architected key components and documentation that balance flexibility with consistency. Working at a scale serving millions of daily visitors, every design decision needed to account for accessibility, performance, and adoption across diverse content needs.

Systems Architecture

Established responsive component hierarchies across content use cases to enable teams to build quickly while maintaining UX and brand consistency. Each component balances strategic flexibility with intentional constraints, ensuring adoptability without sacrificing quality.

Cross-functional Collaboration

Prototyped interactions in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to validate accessibility (WCAG AA) and performance constraints with engineering early. This method of prototyping reduced iteration cycles by catching technical issues in the design phase and , creating a more efficient handoff process.

Multi-Audience Documentation

Created component library documentation optimized for three distinct audiences: developers requiring technical specs, designers needing usage guidelines, and stakeholders tracking adoption. This multi-layered approach reduced implementation friction and enabled self-service adoption across teams.

Impact
  • 20+ Product teams supported
  • Millions Daily visitors served
  • WCAG AA Accessibility compliance
