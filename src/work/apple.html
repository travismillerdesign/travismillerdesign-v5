Apple

Scalable component infrastructure for apple.com

Case Study —

My role

As an embedded designer supporting 20+ product teams across apple.com, I architected core components and documentation frameworks that balance flexibility with consistency. Working at a scale serving millions of daily visitors, every design decision needed to account for accessibility, performance, and adoption across diverse content needs.

Systems Architecture

Established component hierarchy, naming conventions, and governance models that enable teams to build quickly while maintaining brand consistency. Each component balances strategic flexibility with thoughtful constraints, ensuring adoptability without sacrificing quality.

Cross-functional Collaboration

Prototyped interactions in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to validate accessibility (WCAG AA) and performance constraints with engineering early. This reduced iteration cycles by catching technical issues in the design phase rather than during QA, creating a more efficient handoff process.

Multi-Audience Documentation

Created component library documentation optimized for three distinct audiences: designers needing usage guidelines, developers requiring technical specs, and stakeholders tracking adoption. This multi-layered approach reduced implementation friction and enabled self-service adoption across teams.

Impact
  • 20+ Product teams supported
  • Millions Daily visitors served
  • WCAG AA Accessibility compliance
  • Enterprise scale Systematic infrastructure
