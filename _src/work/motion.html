Scotiabank Arena

Motion system design for the downtown Toronto landmark reaching millions of annual viewers. Deployed continuously for 5+ years as permanent brand infrastructure within and around the stadium. Built with customizable controls enabling animation variations and dynamic color schemes for live events including Maple Leafs and Raptors game nights.
